Frequently asked

Simple answers before production starts.

Can you edit videos from footage we already have?

Yes. Existing footage, screen recordings, product clips, photos and brand assets can be structured into a polished edit around the intended message.

Can one creative be adapted for different platforms?

Yes. The creative can be planned with different aspect ratios, durations and supporting variations in mind so the campaign remains consistent.

Do you create advertising concepts as well as edit videos?

Yes. Creative direction can include hooks, messaging structures, visual concepts and variations before the production stage.
